Scope: Windows docked clipboard panel. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: one desktop user recovering text, image, and file copies without losing focus. Task: scan, search, drag, pin, delete, and restore newest-first cards. Constraint: a 190–210 px creek window over any wallpaper; local-only, keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion aware.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Clipboard order becomes a summer creek; refuse the opaque rectangular history sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Turquoise water, rounded stone banks, restrained foliage, thick wooden rafts, parchment/photo/file inserts, and a detached half-width dock.

STORY: Copying launches a raft; users recognize its modality, recover it, and watch ordering remain spatially continuous.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Right-edge creek; stone title/search upstream, three readable rafts in one column, detached controls downstream. Rafts own most width without touching banks.

FORM: Literal creek-and-raft world, user-selected comp, direction seed a21f321a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
